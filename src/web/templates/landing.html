Core Capabilities

Engineered for accuracy and administrative clarity

Everything workforce leaders need to log presence, resolve discrepancies, and understand staffing attendance trends.

Daily attendance and department-wide holidays

Record daily attendance entries by department roster or individual logs, with automated company-wide public holiday scheduling.

Corrections with a complete audit trail

Track every status modification with permanent history recording old status, new status, mandatory reason, operator, and timestamp.

Attendance % and absenteeism % per employee

Evaluate employee attendance percentage and absenteeism rates calculated with mathematical rigor and zero division errors.

Department summaries and ranking

Aggregate departmental performance by total attendance days, avoiding flawed averages of percentages across varying team sizes.

Monthly reports and charts

Access detailed monthly summaries, high-contrast absenteeism ranking tables, and server-side visual trend graphics.

Secure login with Admin and HR roles

Authenticate authorized users with password hashing, automated account lockout protection, and role-based route access controls.

Operational Workflow

Three disciplined steps to complete clarity

A streamlined pipeline that prevents duplicate entries and preserves operational transparency.

1

Record

Input daily attendance by department roster or individual entries, and register company-wide public holidays in atomic database transactions.

2

Correct and audit

Adjust mistaken entries using authorized administrative corrections with mandatory reason notes preserved forever in an append-only audit trail.

3

Analyse

Review real-time employee attendance rates, department performance benchmarks, and monthly trend graphs with complete mathematical accuracy.

Guaranteed Standards

Rules you can trust

Transparent, unyielding business rules designed to ensure workforce fairness and data integrity.

  • Leave is not absenteeism
  • Holidays are excluded from working days
  • A missing record is never counted as an absence
  • No eligible days shows "N/A", never a misleading 0%
  • Employees are never deleted, only deactivated, so history is never lost
Core Attendance Metric Formula Attendance % = Present ÷ (Present + Absent + Leave) × 100
